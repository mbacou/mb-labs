Figure 2. Left) Simulated X-IFU spectrum of an intervening absorber in the Warm-Hot Intergalactic Medium at z=0.4339.
The plot shows the ratio of the X-IFU data of the blazar 1ES 1553+113 with the local best-fitting continuum model, to
highlight the two OVII He-α and He-β absorption lines. The simulation is performed using the best fitting parameters
derived from the long XMM-Newton RGS observations.21 Right) A simulated X-IFU X-ray spectrum of a medium bright
(fluence = 0.4 × 10−6 erg cm−2 ) Gamma-Ray Burst (GRB) afterglow at z = 7, characterized by deep narrow resonant
lines of Fe, Si, S, Ar, Mg, from the gas in the environment of the GRB. An effective intrinsic column density of 2 × 1022
cm−2 has been adopted.

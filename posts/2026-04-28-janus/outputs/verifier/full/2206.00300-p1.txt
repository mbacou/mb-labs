                                                                 equation and the two-phase flow equations using the boundary element method. Using dimensional
                                                                 analysis, we show that interface deformation morphogenesis is universal, i.e. depends on the same
                                                                 dimensionless parameters in acoustics and electromagnetics. We numerically investigate a few se-

                                                                 the leading role of the radiation pressure in morphogenesis of fluid interface deformations and the
                                                                 importance of the propagation-deformation interplay .

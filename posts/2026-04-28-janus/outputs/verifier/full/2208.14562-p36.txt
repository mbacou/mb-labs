    • Production of value-added scientific data products, including catalogues, surveys, and other contributions
      to Level-3 products.
    • As part of the X-ISC daily and long-term instrument performance monitoring, the X-ISC will be responsible
      for the verification, quality control, and validation of all data products, including the quick-look analysis
      (QLA) of products suited for the health monitoring of the instruments.
    • Provision of up-to-date instrument calibration parameters (calibration files)
    • Assisting the SOC in supporting the user community in those cases where in-depth instrument expertise
      beyond SOC capabilities is required.

                                             8. X-IFU CONSORTIUM ORGANISATION
The X-ray Integral Field Unit (X-IFU) and its associated X-IFU Instrument Science Center (X-ISC) will be
provided by an international consortium of ten ESA member states, plus the United States and Japan. Two
other European countries (Ireland and United Kingdom) are providing scientific support to X-IFU.

2 Bimetric Newtonian approximation

A first tentative solution was suggested in 1994 by Petit [9, 13], and later by Henry-
Couannier [14], then Hossenfelder [15]. All are based on a new description of the
Universe, considered as a manifold M4 coupled to two metrics instead of a single
one.

mass structures would be invisible for a positive mass observer (and vice-versa).

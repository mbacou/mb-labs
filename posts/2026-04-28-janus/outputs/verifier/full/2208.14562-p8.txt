1.2 To surpass all other past and future instruments/missions
In the current X-ray science context, the X-IFU has been thought and designed to provide unprecedented
spatially-resolved spectroscopy with performances greatly exceeding those offered by current X-ray observatories
like XMM-Newton and Chandra, or XRISM with its Resolve high resolution spectrometer.24 This capability
comes from an X-ray telescope combining unprecedented collecting area (1.4 m2 at 1 keV), a very good angular
resolution (5-10”) to provide sufficient counts on a 5’ (equivalent diameter) field of view, a better than 2.5 eV
energy resolution below 7 keV, and a sensitive band pass going from 0.2 to 12 keV (see Figure 4)
